What it does

Say it, and it is written

murmur is a small Mac app for dictation. It is free, and it works without internet.

Hold to talk, or tap

Hold Control, Shift and Space while you speak, then let go. Or tap it once to go hands-free, and tap again when you are done.

Private and offline

Speech recognition runs on your Mac. It needs no internet, and nothing you say leaves your Mac.

Fixes itself mid-sentence

Change your mind while you talk. Say “Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday” and murmur keeps Wednesday.

Smart prompt mode

In apps like Claude or ChatGPT, murmur turns a long spoken request into a clean, clear prompt. You can also say “turn this into a prompt” any time.

Your words, your way

Add names and special words to a personal dictionary. Save snippets for things you type often. Pick a different style for each app.

Clear or black recording bar

A small bar shows while you speak. Choose clear glass or black glass to suit your screen.

Install

Install murmur

Download it, open it, drag it into Applications. murmur is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any normal app.

Mac

a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer)

Download for Mac murmur.dmg

Or paste this into Terminal. It downloads, installs and opens murmur in one go.

curl -fsSL https://solomon-69.github.io/Mac-Apps/download/install.sh | bash -s murmur
  1. Open the downloaded file and drag murmur into Applications.
  2. Open murmur from Applications and allow the microphone when your Mac asks.
  3. Click where you want text, hold Control, Shift and Space, speak, and let go.

Questions

Good to know

Does murmur send my voice anywhere?

No. Speech recognition runs on your Mac, so it works with no internet and nothing you say leaves your Mac.

How do I use it?

Hold Control, Shift and Space, speak, then let go. murmur pastes the text where your cursor is. You can also tap once to talk hands-free and tap again to stop.

What if I change my mind while talking?

murmur notices. If you say “Tuesday, no wait, Wednesday”, it keeps Wednesday.

What is Smart prompt mode?

In apps like Claude or ChatGPT, it turns a long spoken request into a clean prompt. Say “turn this into a prompt” to use it in any app.

Can it learn my words?

Yes. Add names and special words to the personal dictionary, save snippets for text you type often, and set a style for each app.

Does it work on every Mac?

murmur needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or newer). It has not been tested on every Mac model yet.

Can I build it myself?

Yes. murmur is open source under the MIT licence. The code and build steps are on GitHub.

Opening the app

murmur is signed and checked by Apple (notarized), so it opens like any normal app: open the download, drag to Applications, double-click. You can check a download against the SHA-256 fingerprints in each release (the SHA256SUMS file) on GitHub.
