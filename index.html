Opening the app

Mac: every app here is signed and checked by Apple (notarized), so it opens like any normal app: unzip, drag to Applications, double-click. Windows: the first time, SmartScreen may warn you: click More info, then Run anyway. You can check a download against the SHA-256 fingerprints in each release (the SHA256SUMS file) on GitHub.
