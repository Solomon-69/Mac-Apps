Mac says it can't verify the app? These apps are free and not signed with a paid Apple certificate, so a browser download gets a warning. Two ways around it: (1) tap copy install line above, open the Terminal app, paste and press Return. It installs and opens the app with no warning. (2) Or open the app once, then go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down and tap Open Anyway next to the app's name.

Already installed? Open Settings in the app and tap Update now. The links always give the newest version.
