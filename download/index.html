Opening a Mac app

Every Mac app here is signed and checked by Apple, so it opens like any other app:

  1. Tap Mac on the app you want. The file downloads straight away.
  2. Open the file, drag the app into Applications, and double-click it.
  3. On Windows, extract the zip first, then open the app inside.

The install line (optional shortcut)

  • One line of text that downloads and installs the app for you.
  • Press Command + Space, type Terminal, press Return.
  • Tap copy on the app's card, click in Terminal, press Command + V, then Return.
  • Wait a few seconds and the app opens. The Mac button does the same thing.

Already installed? Open Settings in the app and tap Update now. The links always give the newest version.
